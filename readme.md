# **Velocity Trials – Game Description**

## **Overview**

**Velocity Trials** is a three-level hybrid adventure game that combines **physical challenges, gesture-based interaction, and immersive virtual gameplay**. Although every participant competes **individually**, all players are present in the same game world, interacting with and competing against one another in real time—similar to a multiplayer survival game. Each player must rely on their own skill, speed, and strategy to progress through every level.

---

# **Level 1 – The Physical Quest**

The game begins with a real-world treasure hunt.

Every participant logs into the game portal, which starts their individual timer. The system assigns the first clue, and players must solve the clue to locate its hidden position.

* **Clue 1** leads to **Clue 2**.
* **Clue 2** leads to **Clue 3**.
* **Clue 3** contains the **Final Secret Code**.

Once the final clue is found, players return to the portal and enter the secret code using **hand gestures** instead of a keyboard. The gesture recognition system validates the input.

### **Qualification**

Players are ranked based on:

* Completion time
* Correct clue sequence
* Successful gesture-based code entry

Only the **top performers** qualify for Level 2.

---

# **Level 2 – The Lost Velocity City**

Qualified players enter a shared futuristic city that has been abandoned after a rogue AI takeover and reclaimed by nature.

All players are inside the **same multiplayer world**, where they can see one another and compete simultaneously, similar to a multiplayer battle-survival experience. However, **every player has their own mission** and must complete it independently.

Each player must:

* Explore the city.
* Avoid dangerous obstacles such as:

  * Rogue Robots
  * Laser Security Systems
  * Virus Zones
  * Oil Spills
  * Environmental Hazards
* **Individually find and collect all three Velocity Crystals** hidden throughout the map.

The crystals are personal objectives—each player must collect their own set of three crystals.

### **Special Powers**

Every player receives one randomly assigned gesture-controlled ability:

* **Sprint** – Move faster.
* **High Jump** – Reach higher platforms.
* **Flight** – Cross difficult areas and obstacles.

These abilities are activated using hand gestures and help players navigate different parts of the city.

### **Qualification for Level 3**

After collecting all three crystals, players are ranked based on:

* Completion time
* Remaining lives
* Overall performance

Only the **top 2 players** advance to the final stage.

---

# **Level 3 – The Final Showdown**

The two finalists enter the **Portal of Destiny** to confront the **Corrupted Overlord**, the rogue AI responsible for the city's downfall.

Before the battle begins, each finalist chooses one legendary weapon:

* **Shadow Sword** – Fast close-range attacks.
* **Gravity Blaster** – Powerful long-range attacks.
* **Elemental Spear** – Balanced combat with elemental abilities.

The finalists must strategically use their weapon, manage their remaining lives, and defeat the Corrupted Overlord before the timer expires.

The player who defeats the boss first—or deals the decisive finishing blow within the time limit—is crowned the **Champion of Velocity Trials** and restores peace to the futuristic world.
